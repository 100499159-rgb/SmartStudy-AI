# SmartStudy-AI

Building AI course project

## Summary

SmartStudy-AI is an artificial intelligence tool designed to help students organize their study time. It creates personalized study plans based on subjects, exam dates, available time and the student's learning needs.

## Background

Many students have difficulties organizing their study time, especially when they have several subjects and exams at the same time. Poor planning can cause stress, procrastination and inefficient studying.

SmartStudy-AI aims to help with problems such as:

* Difficulty deciding what to study first
* Poor time management
* Having several exams or assignments at the same time
* Difficulty maintaining a balanced study schedule

My motivation for this project is to explore how artificial intelligence could help students organize their time and make studying more efficient.

## How is it used?

The student enters information about their subjects, upcoming exams, assignments and available study time.

SmartStudy-AI analyzes this information and creates a personalized study schedule. The system could also update the plan when an exam date changes or when the student completes a task earlier or later than expected.

The main users would be students in secondary school, university or other educational environments.

## Data sources and AI methods

The system would use information provided directly by the student, such as:

* Subjects and topics
* Exam and assignment dates
* Available study hours
* Difficulty of each subject
* Progress on previous study sessions

AI techniques could be used to identify priorities and recommend how the student's available study time should be distributed. The system could learn from previous study sessions and adapt future recommendations to the student's progress.

## Challenges

SmartStudy-AI would not guarantee better academic results. The quality of its recommendations would depend on the information provided by the student.

Another important challenge is privacy, because information about students and their study habits should be stored and processed securely.

The system should also avoid creating unrealistic study schedules or putting excessive pressure on students.

## What next?

In the future, SmartStudy-AI could become a mobile or web application. It could include notifications, progress tracking and integration with calendars and educational platforms.

A more advanced version could learn from the student's study habits and improve its recommendations over time.
